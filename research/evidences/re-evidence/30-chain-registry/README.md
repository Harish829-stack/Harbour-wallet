# 30 — Chain registry (Trust Wallet's 86 enabled chains)

**Source of truth (certain):** `har-balances-chains.json` — response of `GET /v1/balances/chains` captured in `network-log.har`
(stats: total 108, enabled 86, db_lookup_enabled 49, ws_supported 49). Chain ids equal wallet-core `CoinType` values
(verified by `test/chains.test.js` against the byte-identical wallet-core 4.8.2 wasm).
**Joined with:** `../wallet-core/registry.json` (symbol, decimals, explorer, default derivation path, EVM chainId) and
`viem-evm-rpc.json` (public RPC/explorer defaults harvested from viem 2.57.7 chain definitions, fallback only).
**Generated by:** `scripts/gen-chain-registry.mjs` → `src/core/chains/registry.json`; logos by `scripts/fetch-chain-logos.mjs`
(public `trustwallet/assets` repo, bundled into `public/chains/`).

## Findings from the HAR (client behaviour)
- Trust Wallet's client reads balances **per chain from its own node-as-a-service hosts** (`*.twnodes.com/naas/session/<id>/…`):
  Blockbook `api/v2/address/<addr>` for UTXO chains (btc, ltc, doge, dash, bch, zcash, firo, rvn, qtum, dgb, grs, via, dcr), Cosmos LCD
  `cosmos/bank|staking|distribution/v1beta1/...` for Cosmos chains (balances, delegations, unbonding, rewards), Cardano GraphQL,
  Nano/Aion/Callisto/Aeternity/Nebulas/TON JSON-RPC/REST, plus `gateway.us.trustwallet.com/v1/balances/stream` (+ websocket
  `stream/ws`, centrifugo token) for live balances. Harbour does **not** call TW's NaaS/gateway (session-authenticated; proprietary).
- **Deviation (public equivalent):** Harbour queries public endpoints of the same API families (Blockbook/mempool, LCD, JSON-RPC).
  Endpoints are listed per chain in the registry; none could be live-verified from the build sandbox (egress to public RPC blocked).
- `enabled:false` chains in the HAR (litecoin, doge, dash, viacoin, groestlcoin, digibyte, decred, zcash, firo, bitcoincash, nano,
  ravencoin, cryptoorg, aeternity, seievm, cardano, qtum, nebulas, zelcash, evmos, zetaevm, nativeevmos) are **not** in Harbour's
  registry because Trust Wallet disables them in this build.
- Chains with no known public endpoint: `nimiq`, `aion` (balance reports "unsupported", never a fake zero).
