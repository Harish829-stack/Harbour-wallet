Privacy information

Wallet keys and encrypted wallet storage stay within the browser extension. Configured public RPC and market-data providers receive the public addresses or asset queries needed by the requested feature. Their responses and availability may differ.

Help

Keep a secure offline recovery backup. Development status, supported integrations and known gaps are recorded in the project's UI-PARITY.md and INSTALL.md files. Never send a recovery phrase, private key or password in a bug report.
